Amelia Wainwright was in enough trouble before she grew a tail.

A year ago, Amelia’s dad went away on business and never came home. Since then, things haven’t been particularly good at school, and setting off the fire alarm certainly hasn’t helped.

Suspended from school, Amelia finds herself sitting at her dad’s old piano. She plays his favourite tune, just as she’s done countless times before, only this time something rather odd happens: the bookcase opens.

What she discovers behind the bookcase raises a question in Amelia’s mind: how well do I really know my dad?

For someone who sold old books, he seems to have led a remarkably interesting life.

And he might still be alive.

Amelia’s chances of finding him improve considerably when she comes across a watch that allows her to turn into animals.

With the help of her best friend Seb, Amelia sets out to find her dad. Neither of them has much experience in the world of espionage, and Amelia is still getting used to the whole business of being an animal.

With her best friend Seb by her side, Amelia sets out to find her dad. But the more she discovers about his secret life, the more she wonders what he’s got himself into. And, for that matter, what she’s got herself into. After all, being able to turn into an animal isn’t quite as useful as it sounds.

Amelia Wainwright and the Chimera Project is the first book in a new adventure series for readers aged 9–12 and fans of Alex Rider and Animorphs.
